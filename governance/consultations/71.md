# Constitutional consultation

1. **Reversion succeeds.** The diff deletes the complete ENVELOPE tier, the complete Delegated judgment section, and the claim of operator review, merge, direction, governance, and constitutional authority. It also repairs both internal references to the deleted section. Phase 4 remains unimplemented, the gate expressly does not authorize merge, and Model Economy now says standing merge authority “is not claimed here.”
2. **The restored rule is substantively coherent.** Changes to the risk envelope and machine guardrails are again OWNER-ONLY / STOP. Splitting the former single bullet into two does not weaken it, and repository creation remains owner-only. This aligns with FR-CON-001’s prohibition on self-created authority.
3. **The note is candid, not softened.** It identifies PR #53, all three unmet conditions, the ignored warning, the operator’s act of merging, the revert ruling, the absence of retroactive legitimacy, and the requirement that any re-adoption be prospective. “Had no force” accurately states the consequence of invalid adoption rather than laundering it as temporarily valid policy.
4. **No improper rider or self-serving expansion.** The historical note and two reference repairs are within the stated reversion scope. The change removes authority from its author; it introduces no discretionary substitute and no new purported enforcement.
5. **Non-blocking ambiguity.** The pre-existing LOW sentence says work may be “shipped autonomously,” while three more specific clauses deny current merge authority. It does not defeat this reversion, but a later cleanup should replace that bullet with: “- **LOW**: bounded, reversible repository work with green tests may be reviewed autonomously and prepared for integration through the protected PR path; this does not confer merge authority.”
6. **Non-blocking stale references outside CLAUDE.md.** The consultation template and gate comments still say they derive from “CLAUDE.md → Delegated judgment.” The machine gate itself only blocks and confers no authority, so this is documentation debt, not a reason to preserve invalid constitutional text.
7. For literal historical precision, “This is the wording that was in force” could later become “This restores the rule that was in force”; the substance is the same, but the old text was one combined bullet rather than this two-bullet presentation.

No material defect blocks the narrowing change. Requiring these cleanups before reversion would prolong the very authority defect the diff removes.
